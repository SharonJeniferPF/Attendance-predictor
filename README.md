🎓 CanIMiss?

Your attendance knows.

CanIMiss? is a smart student attendance platform that helps students track, predict, and recover attendance.

🔗 Live Demo: https://attendpredic-hi8qawdm.manus.space/

✨ Features

* 📊 Attendance Dashboard — Track attendance at a glance.
* 🔮 Attendance Prediction — See how future classes affect your percentage.
* ⏳ Attendance Time Machine — Explore how past absences impacted your attendance.
* 🚑 Attendance Rescue Mode — Get a personalized plan to recover low attendance.
* 🧮 What-If Simulator — Simulate absences, attendance, OD and medical leave.
* 📈 Visual Insights — Understand your attendance status quickly.

Make attendance management simple, predictive, and actionable for students.
